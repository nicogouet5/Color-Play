Demo privacy and retention

This is Color-Play's public interaction prototype, a proposal for Ripley. It is not operated as a Ripley customer service. Use the demo RUT 11.111.111-1, not a real identifier.

What this page does

  • Validates the RUT format and check digit locally. It uses a per-page salted digest to join demo profiles in memory. Nothing is sent to Ripley, a CRM or any other server.
  • Requests separate, optional permission before camera or uploaded-image color extraction. No face identification or validated skin-tone classification is provided.
  • Keeps image pixels only while reviewing the color test. Moving to tastes discards the image; only a chosen color reference can remain in memory.
  • Uses clothing tastes, optional size and synthetic stock/locations to create recommendations.
  • Creates a genuine QR with only a campaign and random demo ticket token. The QR contains no RUT, image, color profile or size.
  • Records mock purchases, unpurchased shortlist items, a proposed discount tier and an optional Puntos Go preview in this tab only. No real purchase, enrollment or benefit occurs.

Retention and control

No cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, analytics or profile uploads are used. The temporary in-memory profile and demo events disappear on Finish and clear, Restart and clear, reload, tab hide, closing the page or three minutes without input. Returning to RUT entry can deliberately retain the local demo profile until one of those clearing events; returning mode still requires another consented RUT entry.

The sample returning profile is fictional interface fixture data. RUT is an identifier, not authentication; this page must not be used to expose real customer profiles. Printed tickets or saved PDFs remain under your control outside the page.

Before real-world testing

This is a technical demo notice, not an approved retailer privacy policy or a claim of legal compliance. Before using real identifiers, obtain retailer authorization and legal/privacy review covering the controller and contact, specific purposes, lawful basis and explicit consent where required, profile access/identity safeguards, access and withdrawal/deletion procedures, CRM/POS recipients, retention periods and security. The supplied strategy references Chilean Ley21.719; counsel must verify the applicable obligations and dates.

Production loyalty enrollment needs its own terms and permissions. It cannot be inferred from color-test consent. Hardware calibration, algorithm performance and real stock accuracy also need independent validation.
