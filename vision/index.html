    What this prototype measures and what it doesn't

    Classic computer vision, no AI model. We downscale the image and cluster its pixels with k-means in CIELAB. The proportions describe the whole image, background included. A sample uses the average of a small area.

    Lighting, white balance and your screen change colors. We do not detect faces or infer skin tones, personal seasons or sensitive attributes. Palettes and looks are suggestions from an illustrative catalog. The recommender combines rules for style, weather, habitual garments and the colors you confirm; it is not a professional color analysis or a validated purchase recommendation.

    The camera only turns on when you request it. It stops when you capture, cancel or hide the page. Clear session removes the image, answers, references and looks from the application's memory. Downloads are voluntary: the palette file contains colors and the looks file includes your answers and garments. Neither includes your photo.
