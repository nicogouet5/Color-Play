__HEADER_PLAN__

One-Page Business Plan.
Apparel first. Proof before scale.

Customer & buying unit [A]

Client: Chilean fashion retailer. Buyer: Apparel Merchandising / Buying. Champion: Makeup / Beauty. Commercial leadership + Finance decide; CX, experts, Operations, IT/privacy and Procurement influence or gate access. No commitments.

Problem & WHO [O / A]

Initial target [A]: recent fast-fashion shoppers aged 18–28; women recruited. Among n=9 qualifying respondents, 8 choose familiar colors; 7 report discarding a garment at least sometimes [O, E1]. Secondary ages 29–35 remain [A], n=1.

Product promise [A]

Optional human guidance connects an explained palette to 2–3 available tops or shirts. The shopper may disagree, stop or choose no result. First study: portable rack, no photos, stored profiles, CRM, transaction linkage or billing.

Differentiation & evidence [O / A]

Beat familiar choices, trial-and-error and generic seller advice. Proposed edge [A]: explained color guidance connected to available stock. 8/9 rate usefulness highly; 9/9 report try-on intent [O, E2–E3]. Neither superiority nor actual behavior is proven.

Channel & adoption [A]

Direct B2B → assisted B2B2C. Apparel-buyer outreach → demo → scoped case → review → conditional pilot. First learn without retailer systems; a station needs signed access, an Operations owner and privacy/quality gates.

Revenue & price [A]

Retailer pays in a later commercial phase; shopper use stays optional. Test fixed fee, valid-session fee and hybrid. Historical CLP 800/profile + 3% linked sales is an unaccepted scenario only; it cannot fund the no-profile, no-linkage study.

Costs, capacity & viability [A]

Historical scenario: revenue CLP 580 − variable 250 = 330/session; fixed 660,000/month. Breakeven 2,000 sessions vs capacity 660 → −CLP 442,200/month. Not viable; obtain quotes and measure time before pricing.

Assets & next gate [A]

Build study controls; borrow expert and retailer access; buy tools only after quotes; bot reviewed admin, never evidence. Test five shoppers first, then quality, access, willingness to pay and feasible incremental retailer value.

Decision now

Fund learning, not rollout. Continue discovery only if ≥4/5 select or want to try a suggestion, median trust ≥4/5 and ≤1 report pressure. These are proposed directional gates, not proof of commercial lift. Makeup expansion and shade matching remain unvalidated.

Current synthesis: D1–D7 + E1–E5. Detailed economics: D6. Tests and proposed owners: Assumption Log A1–A6.
